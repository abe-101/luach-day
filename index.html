About the luach

A luach (לוח) is the Hebrew word for a calendar or almanac. On the Jewish calendar, each day carries a Hebrew date — a day, a month such as Tishrei, Nisan or Elul, and a year counted from creation. The day begins at nightfall rather than midnight, so today's Hebrew date shown here advances after dark. luach.day computes the current date in your own browser and time zone; no account, no tracking, no app to install.
